Our story

A family promise, kept for seven decades.

In 1954, Sh. Sham Lal Ji opened a small dry-cleaning shop in Sector 16 with a single washing machine — and the unwavering support of his wife, Smt. Radha Rani.

The idea was simple: give Chandigarh a flawless, unstained wardrobe. With the blessings of God and a devoted team, that family business grew into the name the city trusts today.

Our technology

Hydrocarbon cleaning. Gentler on fabric, kinder to the planet.

Our hydrocarbon machines follow an exact, error-free cycle — wash in hydrocarbon solvent, clean, dry with precisely controlled heat, then cool — for everything from office wear to your most delicate pieces.
